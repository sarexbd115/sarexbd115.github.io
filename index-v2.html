About

About SAREX-BD

SAREX-BD is a premium leather goods brand in Bangladesh. We craft wallets, belts, bags and jackets from genuine leather, with careful finishing and honest prices.

Our Story

SAREX-BD was built around one idea: a well made leather piece should last for years and get better with use. Every product is designed to be practical, refined and dependable.

Our Mission

To provide the best leather goods, with uncompromising quality and service, to customers across Bangladesh.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted premium leather brand in Bangladesh, known for craft, quality and customer care.

Our Values

  • Genuine Leather We use genuine leather and are open about what each product is made of.
  • Quality Commitment Careful stitching, finishing and inspection before every order leaves us.
  • Customer Satisfaction Clear information, honest prices and quick help when you need it.

Return & Refund

Return Policy

If your product is defective, tell us within 7 days of delivery. After we verify the defect, we will provide a free return or exchange.

Return Conditions

  • The return request must be made within 7 days of delivery.
  • The product must be unused, in its original packaging, with tags.
  • Returns for personal reasons (not defects) incur a return charge.

Return Process

  • Submit a return request below with your order number and phone number.
  • We contact you to confirm the request.
  • After verification we arrange the return or exchange.

Refund Process

Refund details for each case are confirmed by our team when your return is approved.

Customer Support

For help with returns, call 01610244533 or chat on WhatsApp.

FAQ

How do I place an order?

Choose a product, pick the size and colour, add it to your bag and press Checkout. Fill in your details and press Place Order.

How do I pay?

We accept Cash on Delivery. You pay when you receive your order.

What is the delivery charge?

The delivery charge is shown at checkout, based on your division.

How do I track my order?

Open Track Order and enter your order number and phone number.

What if a product is defective?

Request a return within 7 days of delivery from the Return & Refund page.

How do I choose my size?

Each product page lists the available sizes. Use the Size chart to compare measurements.

What if an item is sold out?

The Sold out label shows when stock is empty. Check back later.

Still need help?

Call 01610244533 or chat on WhatsApp.

Privacy Policy

1. Information we collect

We collect your name, phone number, delivery address and order details when you order or create an account. Your bag, wishlist and language choice are saved on your own device.

2. How we use it

We use your information to process and deliver orders, confirm orders by phone, handle returns and provide customer support.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with delivery partners where needed to deliver your order.

4. Security

Passwords are stored in encrypted form and are never shown. Your sign-in is protected by a private key stored on your device.

5. Your choices

You may ask us to update or delete your account and personal information by contacting us on WhatsApp or by phone.
